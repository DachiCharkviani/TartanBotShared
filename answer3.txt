# Comprehensive Report on 67-272 Application Design and Development: Prerequisites and Course Requirements

## Prerequisites

To enroll in **67-272 Application Design and Development (12.0 units)**, students must satisfy the following prerequisite requirements:

- **15-121 (Introduction to Data Structures)** with a minimum grade of **D**,  
  **OR**  
  **15-122 (Principles of Imperative Computation)** with a minimum grade of **D**
- **67-262 (Database Design and Development)** with a minimum grade of **D**

*“Course 67-272 Application Design and Development (12.0 units) requires, as prerequisites, either 15-121 (at least D) or 15-122 (at least D) and 67-262 (at least D).”*

---

## Course Content / Units

**67-272 Application Design and Development** is a 12.0 unit course. In the context of the concentration, the following requirements apply:

- **Course Units:**  
  - The course itself is 12 units  
  - For concentration completion (see below), students must also complete:
    - A minimum of **three relevant IS courses** (totaling 27 units)
    - **One summative course or approved substitute** (9 units)

- **Required Course Groupings:**  
  To fulfill the concentration, students must take at least:
  - **One course from the Enabling Methods group**
  - **One course from the Cross-Cutting Topics group**
  - **One other relevant IS course**

---

## Completion Requirements

To complete the concentration (which 67-272 may be a part of), students must meet the following conditions:

1. **Prerequisite Completion:**  
    - All prerequisite courses listed above must be completed with at least a grade of D.

2. **Coursework:**
    - Complete a minimum of **three relevant IS courses** (totaling 27 units), which includes:
        - At least **one course** from the **Enabling Methods group**
        - At least **one course** from the **Cross-Cutting Topics group**
        - At least **one other relevant IS course** (this may overlap with 67-272 itself if it fulfills a group requirement)

3. **Summative Course:**
    - Complete a **summative course or its approved substitute** (9 units)

4. **Total Units:**
    - Complete a total of **27 coursework units** (from at least three IS courses as outlined), **plus the summative course** (for a total of at least 36 units)

5. **Corequisite Arrangements:**
    - “Corequisite arrangements [are] possible under exceptional circumstances.” Students who do not meet prerequisites may be allowed concurrent enrollment with approval.

6. **All Requirements Must Be Met for Completion:**
    - “All 27 coursework units and the summative course are required for completion…”

---

## Summary of Requirements

- **Prerequisite Courses:**  
    - 15-121 or 15-122 (min. grade D)
    - 67-262 (min. grade D)
- **Course Group Requirements for Concentration:**  
    - At least one each from: Enabling Methods group, Cross-Cutting Topics group, and another relevant IS course 
    - At least three IS courses in total (minimum of 27 units)
- **Summative Requirement:**  
    - One summative course or approved substitute (9 units)
- **Units Required:**  
    - Total of 27 coursework units + 9 summative course units
- **Enrollment Conditions:**  
    - All prerequisites must be met unless exceptional, approved corequisite arrangement is granted.

---

> **All conditions listed above must be satisfied to enroll in and complete the 67-272 Application Design and Development course and relevant concentration requirements. Omitting any prerequisite, course grouping, unit, or summative requirement will result in incomplete eligibility or fulfillment of the program.**
