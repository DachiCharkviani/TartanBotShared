# Graduation Requirements for B.S. in Information Systems  
(Dietrich College of Humanities and Social Sciences, Carnegie Mellon University)

## Total Units Required
- **360 units** to graduate

---

## Technical Core
- **Mathematics:** 1 course
- **Computer Science:** 3 courses

---

## Curriculum Structure
- **Core Courses:** 8 required
- **Breadth Courses:** 3 required

---

## General Education (GenEd) Requirements
- **Foundations:** 6 requirements
- **Disciplinary Perspectives:** 5 requirements
  - *Logic/Mathematical Reasoning:* Minimum of 9 units from approved catalog (e.g., 21-127, 80-210, 73-155, 73-347, 79-175, 80-312)
- **Special Seminars:** 2 requirements

---

## Minor or Concentration
- **Completion of a Minor or Concentration** is mandatory.

---

## Additional Degree Requirements
- **At least one course from each group:**
  - Technical Core group
  - Regulatory and Behavioral Core group
- **At least one Information Systems (IS) course relevant to the chosen concentration**
- **Concentration requirements:**
  - 3 concentration courses (minimum 27 units)
  - 1 summative/capstone course or approved substitute (9 units)

### Example: Security and Privacy Concentration Courses
- **Section A (choose from):**
  - 67-380 Information Systems Security
  - 67-223 Digital Trace and Identity
  - 67-301 Networks and Telecommunications
  - 15-316 Software Foundations of Security and Privacy
  - 67-367 Information Warfare
  - 67-384 Digital and Cybercrime
  - 67-404 Blockchain Applications
- **Section B (choose from):**
  - 67-313 Digital Risk Management and Auditing
  - 67-415 Cyber Ethics and Regulation
  - 17-334 Usable Privacy and Security
- **Summative course:** 
  - 67-427 Concentration Capstone (Security and Privacy) or approved substitute

---

## Requirements for Credit (Independent Study, Research, Internships)
- Research/project must relate to information systems
- Faculty sponsor must be IS Program-affiliated
- Application due by end of first class week
- Written agreement on assignments, due dates, progress reviews, and grading criteria prior to application

---

## Compliance
- **Every requirement must be satisfied as detailed above** to be awarded the B.S. in Information Systems.
