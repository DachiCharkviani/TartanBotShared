# BS in Biological Sciences Requirements at CMUQ

## Core Biology Courses
- 03-151 Honors Modern Biology (10 units) **or** 03-121 Modern Biology
- 03-201 Undergraduate Colloquium for Sophomores (2 units)
- 03-220 Genetics (9 units) **or** 03-221 Genomes, Evolution, and Disease: Introduction to Quantitative Genetic Analysis (9 units)
- 03-231 Honors Biochemistry (9 units) **or** 03-232 Biochemistry I (9 units)
- 02-250 Introduction to Computational Biology (12 units)
- 03-320 Cell Biology (9 units)
- 03-343 Experimental Techniques in Molecular Biology (12 units)
- 03-344 Experimental Biochemistry (12 units) **or** 03-345 Experimental Cell and Developmental Biology (12 units) **or** 03-346 Experimental Neuroscience (12 units)
- 03-411 Topics in Research (1 unit)
- 03-412 Topics in Research (1 unit)

## Chemistry Sequence
- 09-105 Introduction to Modern Chemistry **or** 09-107 (earning an A in 09-107 waives 09-106)
- 09-106 Modern Chemistry II
- 09-217 Organic Chemistry I
- 09-218 Organic Chemistry II
- 09-207 Techniques in Quantitative Analysis
- 09-208 Techniques in Organic Synthesis and Analysis

## Biology Electives
- Six 03-xxx courses at the 300-level or above (≥18 units)
    - Excludes 03-445/03-545
    - Up to three may be interdisciplinary electives
    - Up to 36 units of 03-445/03-545 research may count toward this requirement
    - Cross-registration permitted with prior approval

## General Education & Core Seminars
- EUREKA! Seminar
- Third-Year Seminar
- Engage in Wellness I, II, III
- One Cultural/Global elective
- Four non-technical electives
- Free electives as needed to reach at least 360 total units

> **Note:** For course sequencing, double-counting rules, and transfer credit approvals, students must consult their Biological Sciences advisor.
