# Information Systems Course 67-262: Database Design and Development – Prerequisite Information

## Official Prerequisite (per course catalog)
- You must have earned at least a 'D' grade in any one of the following courses **prior to enrolling**:
    - 15-112: Fundamentals of Programming and Computer Science
    - 02-120: Programming for Scientists
    - 15-122: Principles of Imperative Computation
    - 15-121: Introduction to Data Structures

## Equivalent Experience & Exceptions
- If you have not completed the exact listed courses but have **equivalent programming experience**, you should:
    - Consult with the IS academic advisor.
    - Check the official course registration system for current prerequisite policies.

## Advising Documents and Curriculum Placement
- No explicit course prerequisite for 67-262 is listed in the available advising documents.
- 67-262 is scheduled for the second-year fall semester in the curriculum.
- It is preceded by 67-250: The Information Systems Milieux (taken in first-year spring) in the typical sequence, suggesting:
    - 67-250 may serve as an **informal prerequisite** or recommended background, although this is not a formal requirement.

## Verification for Special Cases
- For students with unique academic backgrounds or using equivalent coursework:
    - Direct verification with the IS academic advisor or review of the official registration system is recommended to confirm current prerequisites.

## Summary
- **Concrete requirement:** 'D' or better in one of 15-112, 02-120, 15-122, or 15-121.
- **Strongly recommended:** Background in 67-250, though not formally required.
- **Action for nonstandard circumstances:** Contact advisor and consult the registration system for possible exceptions or updates.
