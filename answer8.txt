# Information Systems Course 67-373 Requirements

## Course Title and Role
- **67-373: Information Systems Consulting Project**
    - A 12-unit core course.
    - Mandatory for all Information Systems majors.

## Core Curriculum Requirement
- **67-373** must be completed as part of the Information Systems Core.
- The complete set of required core courses is:
    - 67-100
    - 67-200
    - 67-250
    - 67-262
    - 67-272
    - 67-373
    - 17-313
    - (Choose one: 95-422 or 67-382)
    - (Choose one: 05-391, 05-410, 05-452, or 05-897)
- All courses listed above (including 67-373) must be successfully completed to fulfill the core requirement for the major.

## Prerequisites and Additional Requirements
- There are **no additional published prerequisites** for 67-373 itself, aside from its inclusion as a required core course.
- No further specific requirements for enrollment in 67-373 are mentioned in the official course catalog or advising documentation.

## Summary
- **67-373 is mandatory** as part of the Information Systems Core for majors.
- **No special prerequisites** beyond being an Information Systems major and completing the listed core courses.
- Completion of **all core courses**, including 67-373, is required for the Information Systems degree.
