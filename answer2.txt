Course Code: 67-272  
Course Name: Application Design and Development  
Units: 12.0

Course Description  
This course prepares students in the design and development of software applications, emphasizing user-centered design. Students will develop a prototype web application as a major project. Core learning includes designing relational databases, rapid acquisition of new programming languages, employing the Model-View-Controller (MVC) software pattern, ensuring technical quality, and applying user-centered design principles. The course is a required component of the Information Systems core.

Prerequisites  
- One of the following programming courses with a minimum grade of D:
  - 15-122 "Principles of Imperative Computation"
  - 15-121 "Introduction to Data Structures"
AND
- 67-262 "Database Design and Development" with a minimum grade of D

Corequisites  
None.

Anti-Requisites  
None.

Required Prior Knowledge

- Completion of introductory database design and development (from 67-262)
