# Graduation Requirements for a Bachelor's Degree in Computer Science

## Total Units
- **360 units required to graduate.**

## Core Requirements
- **Core@CMU:** 99-103 units required.

## Computer Science Courses
- **Core CS Courses:** Complete 7 out of 9 core CS courses.
- **Constrained CS Electives:** Take 4 electives, one each from the following domains:
  - Domains
  - Artificial Intelligence (AI)
  - Logic & Languages
  - Systems
- **General CS Electives:** 2 electives, each at least 9 units (independent study or senior thesis may substitute).

## Mathematics and Communication
- **Math:** Complete 6 out of 7 required Math courses.
- **Technical Communication:** 1 course required.

## General Education
- **Science/Engineering:**
  - Take 4 courses, each at least 9 units.
  - Two courses must be from the same science/engineering department.
  - At least one must be a lab course (if less than 9 units, such as Chem 101, still requires 4 science courses beyond labs).
  - Astronomy with lab (courses 33-124 and 33-126) does NOT count for this requirement.
- **Humanities/Arts:** 63 units (approximately 7 courses) required.

## Ethics
- **Ethics Course:** Required for graduation.

**All of the above requirements must be completed to be eligible for graduation with a bachelor's degree in Computer Science.**
