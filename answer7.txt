# Business Administration Policies

## Additional Major in Business Administration
- Applicants must complete the Tepper Business Administration minor, course 70-207 (Probability & Statistics for Business) or equivalent, and course 21-120 (Differential & Integral Calculus) with a minimum grade of C.
- Additional major students follow primary major grading and academic policies.
- Upon completion, the additional major is certified and appears on the diploma.

## Concentration Requirement (Primary Majors)
- Business Administration majors must complete one concentration and the specified number of business elective units as stated in the catalog.
- Students must select a concentration in Stellic by the end of sophomore year.
- Concentration and business electives may not be double-counted towards other Business Administration requirements, including the minor.

## Additional Major Holders
- Students with a Business Administration additional major have the minor and concentration requirements waived.
- They must complete at least 27 units of business elective courses.

## Product Management Majors
- Product Management majors with an HCII minor or additional major have their HCII course requirements waived.
- They must complete one additional Tepper elective in Product Management.

## Course Double Counts
- No double counting is permitted between concentration/elective requirements and other Business Administration requirements.
- Double counting with other departments (such as minors, additional majors, or dual degrees) is governed by that department’s policies.
- No double counting is allowed between minors and home-college general education requirements.
- Non-Tepper minors and/or additional majors follow their home college’s catalog rules.
- For multiple Tepper minors, only one course may be double counted across minors.

## Minor & Additional Major Options
- Business Administration majors must complete a minor; an additional major can satisfy this requirement for some.
- Each college administers its declaration process.
- Upon declaration, students must meet regularly with both their minor/additional major advisor and the Tepper advisor.
- Completion of an additional major waives the minor and concentration requirements, but at least 27 units of business electives must still be completed.
- The minor or additional major must be certified by the relevant department before graduation; lack of certification prevents graduation.

## Pass Grades
- A maximum of nine “P” (Pass) units may count toward the 364-unit Business Administration requirement (excluding Core@CM).
- Spring 2020 P/N exceptions are determined by university policy.
