# Prerequisites for Information Systems Course 67-272 (Application Design and Development)

## Course Prerequisites
- You must have completed either **15-122** or **15-121** with at least a grade of D.
- You must have completed **67-262** with at least a grade of D.

## Additional Requirements for Related Groups of Courses
- Complete at least **one course** from the enabling methods, techniques, and tools group of courses.
- Complete at least **one course** from the cross-cutting topics group of courses.
- Complete at least **one IS course** relevant to the chosen concentration.
- Complete **3 courses in total** (equaling 27 units).
- Complete a **summative course** or an equivalent approved substitute (9 units).
